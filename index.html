Practise being asked, not being perfect.

Nobody expects a finished speech. The interviewers are listening for one real story and one clear idea. Warm up here, then have a go at the real thing.

The only three rules

One real story beats five opinions. Who was there, what you did, what happened after.

Short is strong. About 30 seconds per answer. If you can't finish it in a breath, it's too long.

Pausing is allowed. "That's a good question, can I think for a second?" is a good answer in itself.

How the scoring works. The app checks the shape of your answer — did you name a real thing, say what you'd do, keep it short. It can't judge whether your story is good. Copy your answers at the end and your coach will rate the content properly.
